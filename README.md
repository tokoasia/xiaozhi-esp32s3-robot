---

## 📋 Spesifikasi Kit
- **Microcontroller:** ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 DevKitC-1 (16MB Flash, 8MB Octal PSRAM)
- **Audio Output:** MAX98357A I2S Class D Audio Amplifier + Speaker 3W
- **Audio Input:** INMP441 Omnidirectional I2S Digital Microphone
- **Display:** 0.96 inch SSD1306 OLED (I2C)
- **Konektivitas:** WiFi 2.4GHz + WebSocket ke XiaoZhi Cloud AI Service

---

## ⚡ Cara Cepat Flash Firmware
1. Hubungkan ESP32-S3 ke PC dengan kabel data Type-C.
2. Gunakan **Web Flasher** langsung di browser: [store.tokoasia.me](https://store.tokoasia.me)
3. Pilih firmware `xiaozhi-ai-voice` dan klik **Connect & Flash**.
